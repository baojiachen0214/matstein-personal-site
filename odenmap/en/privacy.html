PRIVACY

Privacy policy

How the OdenMap app and this website handle information. App notes are based on the macOS project documentation published October 22, 2026. The iPhone and iPad beta is also built around local documents; platform permissions and preview behaviour may differ, and the in-app notes govern the beta.

Website notes updated November 19, 2026 · Developer: Jiachen Bao

Your documents stay under your control

OdenMap does not require an OdenMap account and does not automatically upload your mind maps or attached resources to a developer-operated server. Documents are saved where you choose. Preferences, resource references, previews and working caches are stored locally as needed for editing. OdenMap does not include developer-operated advertising, behavioural analytics or automatic crash-reporting services.

If you save a document in iCloud Drive or another synced folder, synchronization is handled by that provider under its terms and privacy policy. External file links may require access to the original location. These are not OdenMap-operated cloud storage services.

When you take part through TestFlight, Apple processes beta information, feedback and diagnostics under TestFlight's terms.

Optional system integrations

Calendar and Reminders access is requested only for the corresponding features. With your permission on macOS, OdenMap reads and writes the items needed for your chosen associations, such as titles, notes, dates, alarms and links. Those items may be synced by your system accounts. You can revoke access in System Settings. Existing items remain in the system apps until you remove them there.

When you search for a reminder location, the entered place query is sent to Apple's map service. This feature does not require OdenMap to request the device's current location.

External content and links

HTML or Markdown resources can contain remote images, fonts, scripts or other external content. Previewing such resources can contact their providers, revealing information such as your IP address and requested content. WebKit previews may keep website data according to the preview mode. Opening a link uses the relevant system app or browser. The external service's privacy policy applies; do not preview untrusted content.

System diagnostics

macOS, iOS and Apple may collect or share diagnostics according to your system settings and Apple's privacy policy.

This website and support

The website includes no developer-added analytics, tracking cookies, ads or third-party fonts. Your appearance choice is saved in this browser's local storage; language is part of the page address. The site does not read or upload local files. Hosting infrastructure may retain necessary access logs for security and troubleshooting.

Project feedback can be sent to odenmap_support@yeah.net. Do not send sensitive information or private documents.

Retention and choices

Saved documents remain until you delete them from their saved locations. You can remove resources, delete local files and revoke system permissions. Removing OdenMap does not automatically remove documents saved outside its container or items created in Calendar and Reminders. Local caches and website data can remain while the app is installed.

For a general privacy inquiry, or a request concerning information you voluntarily posted, email odenmap_support@yeah.net without adding personal details.

Changes and contact

Updates will be published here with a revised effective date. Contact the developer at odenmap_support@yeah.net. No telephone support is provided.
